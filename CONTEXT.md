# Girls Band Live ubiquitous language

## Archive

The public-facing collection of girls-band live events, related works, and
supporting metadata.

## Live

A scheduled or archived live event. A Live can have bands, formats, ticket
sales, ticket tiers, and publication state.

## Band

The performing group associated with a Live and its source work or series.

## Work and Series

A Work is an anime, game, or other source property. A Series groups related
works or installments for navigation and administration.

## Vendor

An external ticket or event provider, identified by its domain and used to
link outbound purchase or event information.

## Publication state

The administrative state that determines whether a Live is available through
the public archive. Draft and published are distinct from the event's date.
